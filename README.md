## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 27, 2026, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a third-person action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Focus Entertainment. Set fifteen years before A Plague Tale: Requiem, it follows Sophia across the mysterious Minotaur's Island.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy**. This single-player PC trainer provides optional assistance for Sophia's melee combat, stealth encounters, exploration, and dangerous island trials. It is designed around the game's parries, dagger and sword attacks, grappling hook, enemy encounters, and light-based environmental puzzles.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed story experience, easier melee encounters, additional help with stealth sections, or support while exploring the Minotaur's Island and solving its ancient light-based puzzles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents Sophia from taking normal combat and environmental damage.
- **Infinite Health** — Keeps Sophia's health filled during exploration and encounters.
- **Infinite Consumables** — Prevents supported consumable resources from decreasing when used.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Enemy Freeze** — Temporarily prevents selected enemies from moving or attacking.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows successful attacks to defeat standard enemies with a single hit.
- **Unlimited Grappling Hook Uses** — Allows repeated use of Sophia's grappling hook without exhausting supported charges.
- **Easy Parry Window** — Expands the timing window for successful defensive parries.

### 🌍 World

- **Stealth Assistance** — Reduces enemy awareness while Sophia hides, relocates, or moves through guarded areas.
- **Puzzle Assist** — Provides assistance during light-manipulation puzzles involving the stolen Minoan sphere.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move Sophia normally through the island and use cover, hiding places, and traversal routes to avoid danger. Stealth Assistance is useful when repositioning around patrols. Disable combat-related options before scripted movement or transition sequences.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage Sophia's normal equipment and resources through the game's own interface. Activate Infinite Consumables only while using supported items, and avoid changing inventory values during menus, loading screens, or cutscenes.

### World & Environment

Explore the island, follow clues recorded in Sophia's notebook, and investigate paths connected to the Minoan era. Puzzle Assist can reduce the difficulty of light-based mechanisms without changing the intended exploration route or story progression.

### Combat & Weapons

Sophia relies on agility, dagger and sword attacks, precise parries, attack combinations, and her grappling hook. Use Easy Parry Window for defensive timing, Enemy Freeze to control crowded encounters, and One-Hit Defeat when you want to shorten battles. God Mode provides the safest option during difficult trials.
